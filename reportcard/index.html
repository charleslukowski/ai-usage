How the grade is set

One question: can a client read your money with the key you already have?
Anative

Serves an ai-usage manifest at a discovery path, with your normal credential.

Bclean

No manifest, but a clean balance or spend read with your normal key. A trivial adapter.

Cawkward

Readable, but needs a separate admin key, hand-built GraphQL, or a bolt-on product.

Dheaders

No usage endpoint — only response headers or 429 bodies hint at what is left.

Fdark

No programmatic path at all. Log in to a dashboard, read it with your eyes.

For providers — an A is a 20-line endpoint

Serve GET /.well-known/ai-usage.json behind your normal auth, return the balance and spend you already compute for your own dashboard, and you move from the red row to the badge. You already have the data. Your users are already asking.

Methodology. Providers are graded on whether account balance and month-to-date spend are readable over an authenticated API with your normal credential, as of July 2026. Grades are our assessment of the public API surface, not the providers' self-reports.

Live-checked (2026-07-18). OpenRouter, fal.ai, and Anthropic were probed against real accounts — those three grades reflect what a standard key actually returned. OpenRouter read balance + spend cleanly; fal.ai's billing route returned 403 without a billing-scoped key; Anthropic returned month-to-date spend via a separate Admin key.

This is a draft artifact for review, not a published index. Figures shown in the spec examples are illustrative. Provider APIs change — a grade here is a snapshot, not a promise.
